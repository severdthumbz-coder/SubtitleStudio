ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin: the Silero VAD v6.2.0 speech detector, converted to ggml by the whisper.cpp
project (https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/whisper-vad). Built into Subtitle Studio and used by
AI Transcribe to find the speech before Whisper listens. SHA-256:
